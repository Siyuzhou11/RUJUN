About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Texas A&M University, specializing in Generative AI and Large Language Models for automatic assessment in engineering education. My research integrates cutting-edge LLM technologies, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and multi-agent systems to develop intelligent educational platforms that automate grading, tutoring, and personalized learning at scale.

I am also the Co-founder, CEO & CTO of Encando.AI, an AI-powered higher education SaaS platform serving 2000+ active users across multiple universities including Texas A&M, Texas Tech, UNT, and UT Austin. By bridging fundamental AI research with practical deployment, I aim to transform education through scalable, intelligent automation and support evidence-based decision-making in teaching and learning.

Selected Research Projects

GAI-Powered ADU Planner
🏆 1st Place - 2024 RAG Hackathon (70+ projects, LlamaIndex & DataStax)
Team Lead | February 2024

Developed a Generative AI-powered application where users input their property address to receive customized accessory dwelling unit (ADU) plans based on property layout and state building policies. The system streamlines the ADU process by providing nearby suppliers and floorplan options aligned with construction requirements, including visual rendering capabilities.

Technologies: RAG, LlamaIndex, Vector Search, Multi-modal AI

ChaTA - LLM-Powered Teaching Assistant Agent
🏆 1st Place ($20,000) - 2023 CMU GenAI Innovation Incubator Hackathon
June 2023 - July 2023

Developed an in-house AI tutor fine-tuned on 10,000 Q&A pairs based on the LLaMA-2 model. Created a custom course database to augment the fine-tuned model's performance with information retrieval technology. ChaTA demonstrates proficiency in accurately answering a broad spectrum of student questions related to their coursework.

Technologies: LLaMA-2, Fine-tuning, Information Retrieval, Custom Database

Automated Grading System for Engineering Education
Ongoing Research | 2021 - 2025

Developed an automated grading system for evaluating open-ended questions in mechanical engineering quizzes/exams based on transformer-based open-source large language models. The system demonstrates high accuracy in conceptual question evaluation and has been deployed in actual classroom settings.

Technologies: Transformer Models, BERT, GPT, PyTorch, Prompt Engineering
